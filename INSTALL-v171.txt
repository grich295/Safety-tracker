SAFETY TRACKER v1.7.1 — UPGRADE FROM v1.6.1
============================================

IMPORTANT CORRECTION IN v1.7.1
------------------------------
Your existing Force Sync & Review is already working. This build deliberately leaves it alone.
The v1.7.1 patch does NOT create a Force Sync button, replace its handler, intercept its click,
disable it, or change its status text.

WHAT THIS BUILD CHANGES
-----------------------
1. Documents becomes the controlled source library.
   - RA, COSHH, SSW, SDS/MSDS, policies and procedures remain controlled documents.
   - Normal staff learning/sign-off is managed from Training rather than assigning source documents directly.

2. Training becomes the learning/sign-off area.
   - Risk Assessment -> self-training.
   - COSHH Assessment -> self-training.
   - Safe System of Work -> instructor-led.
   - Toolbox Talk -> instructor-led.
   - Existing induction, refresher, ad-hoc and other training remain supported.

3. Controlled RA/COSHH/SSW files create matching auto-managed Training records.
   - Training points at the current source-document version.
   - A new controlled version creates a fresh active training record while retaining historic evidence.
   - Existing active source-document assignments are copied to Training and deactivated, not deleted.

4. Linking is clearer and more automatic.
   - Link search displays full reference + title + type instead of only a generic SDS/type label.
   - Training can link directly to controlled documents.
   - Newly uploaded/recent PDFs are automatically scanned for RA/COSHH/SSW/PROC references after refresh.
   - Existing document relationships are copied into the matching auto-managed Training record.
   - Conservative SDS <-> COSHH matching remains available.

5. Date defaults.
   - New document issue date defaults to the upload/current date.
   - Controlled-document review date defaults to one year after issue/upload.
   - New training review date defaults to one year after creation.
   - SDS/MSDS remains reference-only with no mandatory one-year review date.

6. Training frequency is editable.
   - One-off, 3, 6, 12, 24 months or Custom.
   - Frequency changes can be applied to current active assignees.

7. Backup includes training-document links.

INSTALL
-------
A. Make a Full Backup ZIP using your current working Safety Tracker.

B. In Supabase -> SQL Editor run:
   001-safety-tracker-v171.sql
   The migration is idempotent. It is safe if the earlier v1.7.0 schema migration was already run.

C. Add app-v171-patch.js beside app-v161.js and bulk-import-v161.js.

D. At the bottom of index.html use:
   <script src="app-v161.js"></script>
   <script src="bulk-import-v161.js"></script>
   <script src="app-v171-patch.js"></script>

E. Replace sw.js with the supplied v1.7.1 service worker.

F. Publish the changed files and refresh the app. A manager/admin refresh performs the v1.7.1
   structural synchronisation and recent-upload link scan automatically.

G. Leave your existing Force Sync & Review exactly as it is.

NOTES
-----
- This build does not delete controlled documents, old assignments, signatures or training evidence.
- Historical document sign-offs remain available even after active RA/COSHH/SSW assignments move to Training.
- Do not clear Documents/history until the new Training structure and links have been checked.
